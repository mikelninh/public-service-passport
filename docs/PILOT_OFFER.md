# Public Service Passport — Institutional Proof Sprint

Initial commercial offer to validate with public-service teams and delivery partners.

## Offer

**Citizen → Evidence → Preflight Proof Sprint**

A fixed-scope engagement that maps one public-service journey into the Public Service Passport case/evidence model and demonstrates the citizen and authority views without pretending to replace the competent authority.

## Deliverables

- citizen journey map;
- current evidence/repeated-data map;
- one bounded Citizen Case schema;
- source/trust-state map;
- deterministic preflight/exception rules for the agreed scope;
- authority-preview integration or synthetic workbench;
- privacy/data-flow review;
- 20–50 controlled golden/edge cases;
- impact and assurance report;
- next-step integration proposal.

## Initial price hypothesis

**€5,000 fixed** for one service journey and one institutional team, excluding unusual third-party system integration.

This is a commercial hypothesis to test, not established market pricing.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- citizen-facing orientation remains free;
- no percentage of benefits or public money;
- no automatic binding decision;
- no claim that self-attested facts are source-verified;
- no personal-data resale;
- unsupported/unknown cases fail visibly.

## Paid expansion

After a successful proof sprint, legitimate paid work includes maintained rule/source packs, integrations, hosted proof verification, operational support, assurance and bounded workflow volume.
