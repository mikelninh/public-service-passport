# Sales Truth Boundary

Sales material may say:

- Public Service Passport helps supported citizens orient, plan evidence and prepare bounded public-service cases;
- the Authority Workbench and 100-case pilot are synthetic demonstrations of preflight/exception routing;
- OpenProof has real local Midnight infrastructure evidence while the current citizen input remains self-attested;
- institutional proof sprints can map one service journey and measure preflight/repeated-evidence friction.

Sales material may not say:

- the product issues a binding entitlement decision;
- a local Authority Preview submits anything to a real authority;
- current citizen self-attested values are cryptographically verified;
- the synthetic authority pilot is measured real-government performance;
- citizens must pay or surrender extra data to discover supported rights.
