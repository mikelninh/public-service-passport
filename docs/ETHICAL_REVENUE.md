# Ethical Revenue Model — Public Service Passport

Status: **v1 public-pilot commercial covenant**.

The product should be financially sustainable without making access to rights harder or turning citizen data into the product.

## Who should pay

**Citizens should not pay to discover, understand or prepare access to basic public rights.**

The natural payers are institutions that receive measurable operational value:

- municipalities and public authorities;
- welfare and benefits-advice organisations;
- public-service delivery partners;
- system integrators and software vendors that need a maintained citizen-case / evidence / proof interface;
- organisations running governed pilots, assurance or interoperability programmes.

## What is legitimately billable

Institutional customers may pay for:

1. deployment and integration;
2. maintained, versioned policy/rule packs with source traceability;
3. workflow configuration and adapter work;
4. assurance, security and release evidence;
5. hosted proof verification / receipt infrastructure;
6. support, uptime and operational monitoring;
7. bounded case-processing capacity where the unit is a completed/verified workflow, not citizen vulnerability;
8. evaluated pilots and evidence reports.

## What we do not monetise

- no percentage of a citizen's benefit, grant, reimbursement or aid;
- no paid tier required to discover a basic entitlement route;
- no sale or advertising use of household, identity, financial or proof data;
- no affiliate steering toward private substitutes for public rights;
- no fee for a safety stop, failed verification or rejected/blocked case;
- no claim that payment upgrades self-attested facts or creates an authority decision.

## Incentive test

A pricing model is rejected if revenue increases when:

- a citizen receives less support;
- more personal data is collected than the workflow needs;
- a case is made artificially complex;
- a safety gate is bypassed;
- an authority or citizen is pushed toward unnecessary action.

## Preferred commercial model

For early pilots:

`fixed discovery/pilot fee + implementation fee + transparent support/hosting`

For mature deployments:

`annual institutional licence/support + integration + optional verified-workflow volume`

Any volume unit must exclude blocked, failed and purely exploratory requests.

## Public-interest architecture

The Citizen Case vocabulary, proof/receipt formats and safety invariants should remain inspectable. The business should earn money from **reliable operation, integration, maintenance and assurance**, not from owning the citizen's identity or trapping public rules behind a proprietary data moat.

North-star commercial metric:

> **Institutional cost/friction removed while citizen access, privacy and decision quality stay the same or improve.**
