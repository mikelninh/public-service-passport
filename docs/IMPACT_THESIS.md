# Impact Thesis

If Public Service Passport works, a person should need less procedural knowledge and disclose less repeated information to reach the right public-service action, while authorities receive better structured cases and clearer exceptions.

The product is successful only when both sides improve:

- citizen: less confusion, repetition and unnecessary disclosure;
- institution: less avoidable manual preflight and clearer provenance;
- authority: decision responsibility remains intact;
- public: policy/source assumptions remain inspectable.

This is the impact contract that commercial metrics must not override.
