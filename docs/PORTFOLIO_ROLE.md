# Portfolio Role — Public Service Passport

Public Service Passport is the citizen-facing front door of the broader trustworthy-public-service stack.

Its job is deliberately narrow:

1. help a person understand relevant supported routes;
2. show what evidence is missing;
3. let the person control reusable facts/evidence;
4. request minimum-disclosure proofs where an accepted issuer exists;
5. prepare a bounded case for an authority workflow without pretending submission or decision occurred.

It should not become:

- a central identity database;
- a shadow benefits authority;
- a payment intermediary taking a cut of public support;
- a generic AI chat layer over every government service.

Commercially, the citizen-facing core is the public-good surface. Institutions pay for maintained rules, integration, assurance, proof verification and workflow operations.
